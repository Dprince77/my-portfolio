01. About

Engineer by training, community operator by practice.

I'm a Software Engineering student at Abiola Ajimobi Technical University in Ibadan, currently interning as a software engineer at HS Advanced Technology and Globus Bank. My day-to-day involves building and maintaining client websites, mostly in WordPress, with some AI feature integration. I also coordinate with senior developers on the right solution for each customer.

On the community side, I'm a Responsible Administrator for OneState RP's Glendale server, part of the game's AFR region, a 57,000+ member social roleplay community region-wide. I manage a 35-person staff team, run weekly meetings, and report directly to the region's Lead Administrator and Community Manager. Separately, I'm Community Manager for Chillgaming, a 100+ member gaming Discord, where I work with the founder on structure and growth.

I'm fluent in English and understand and speak some Arabic. I'm comfortable moving between a technical standup and a moderator check-in. That combination is what I bring to any team.
