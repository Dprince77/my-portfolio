Engineer by training,
community operator by practice.

I'm a Software Engineering student at Abiola Ajimobi Technical University in Ibadan, with a track record that bridges two things people usually keep separate, shipping real software, and running real communities at scale.

On the technical side, I recently shipped AATU-SDCS, a full-stack case management system built with React, Vite and TypeScript on the frontend and Supabase (PostgreSQL + PL/pgSQL).On the backend, handling incident intake, evidence tracking and hearing schedules for a real institutional workflow. Before that, I interned at HIIT Plc, where I designed and built a company website that went on to represent them at a regional competition.

On the community side, I'm a Responsible Administrator for OneState RP's African regional server, a 57,000+ member social roleplay community where I work alongside AFR-region Community Managers, help coordinate a 200+ person moderation team, and author weekly and monthly reports for senior leadership. Separately, I co-founded Chillgaming, a Discord community I built from zero alongside a colleague and have grown to roughly 500 members.

I'm fluent in English and Arabic, and comfortable moving between a technical standup and a moderator escalation call, that combination is what I bring to any team.
