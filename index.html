Predictive Stress Classification Model

A CSC 309 Artificial Intelligence project that classifies stress levels from sleep and vital-sign readings such as snoring rate, respiration, body temperature, blood oxygen, limb movement, REM sleep and heart rate. I trained and compared three models on 630 patient records with an 80/20 train-test split (504 train, 126 test), removed duplicates and discretized the data for the Bayesian network. I also presented the work with correlation heatmaps, a decision-tree view, a network diagram and an AdaBoost error-reduction chart.

03. Support Practice

How I handle player tickets.

These are practice tickets I wrote while teaching myself Zendesk and Jira. They are examples of my approach, not real customer data. Each one moves from the player's first message to a resolution, with escalation to the right team when needed.

Account accessPriority: High

Cannot log in after changing phone

PlayerI got a new phone and now I cannot log in. It says my account does not exist. I have been playing for two years and I am scared I lost everything.
My replyHi, thank you for reaching out, and I am sorry for the scare. Your progress is very likely safe. Could you tell me which sign-in method you used before (Google, Apple or email), and the email linked to your account? Please do not create a new account in the meantime, as that can make recovery harder. Once I have this, I will check the account on my side and update you.
Resolution note: Asked for sign-in method and linked email. Advised against creating a new account. Linked to the correct account and confirmed the login method with the player. Solved.
BillingPriority: High

Charged twice for the same pack

PlayerI bought a gem pack and was charged two times but only received one pack. Please refund the second charge.
My replyHi, I am sorry about the double charge, and thank you for the details. Could you send a screenshot of both charges from your bank or store receipt, with the dates? I will check your purchase history and pass this to our billing team if a duplicate is confirmed. I will update you within the timeframe on our refund policy.
Resolution note: Requested proof of both charges. Checked purchase history, confirmed duplicate, escalated to billing with receipts attached. Player told what to expect. Pending billing, then solved.
Bug reportPriority: Normal

Game crashes when opening the shop

PlayerEvery time I tap the shop the game closes. Started after the last update.
My replyHi, thanks for letting us know. To help our developers fix this quickly, could you tell me your device model, OS version, and the game version number from the settings page? Does it happen every time or only sometimes? In the meantime, please try restarting your device and reinstalling the latest version.
Resolution note: Collected device, OS and app version and steps to reproduce. Checked for similar reports, then logged one clear bug ticket for the dev team (linked in the Jira board). Player informed that it was escalated. Open.
Out of scopePriority: Normal

Player reports another player

PlayerAnother player keeps sending me insulting messages in chat. I have screenshots. Please do something.
My replyHi, I am sorry you went through that, and thank you for telling us. Player conduct reports are handled by our State Assist team, not by technical support. Please open a ticket in our Discord server and attach your screenshots, the other player's username and the approximate time, so the moderators can review it. In the meantime, you can block that player. If you have any technical issue with the game itself, please reply here and I will help.
Resolution note: Outside technical support scope. Redirected the player to open a Discord ticket with the moderation team and told them what to include. Closed with a note that technical issues are still welcome here.
